## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- Zero-dependency Socket.IO client for Dart & Flutter (Engine.IO v4 /
  Socket.IO v4).
- WebSocket transport with automatic heartbeat (server ping / client pong).
- `connect`, `on`, `off`, `emit`, and `emitWithAck` APIs.
- Automatic reconnection with exponential backoff.
- Pure Dart; works on every Flutter platform.
